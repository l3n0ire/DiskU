# Changelog

## v1.0.5

- **DiskU for Windows.** DiskU now runs natively on Windows 10 and 11, with the same treemap, size-sorted sidebar, and inspector as the Mac app. Deleting an item moves it to the Recycle Bin.
- **Clearer downloads.** Each release now offers `macos`, `windows-x64`, and `windows-arm64` zips.

## v1.0.4

- **Update notices.** DiskU checks GitHub for a newer release when it opens and shows a banner linking to it.

## v1.0.3

- **Scan Full Disk.** A new Full Disk button in the toolbar, on the start screen, and in the File menu scans your whole startup disk.
- **Faster sidebar for big folders.** Opening a folder with thousands of items no longer freezes DiskU. The sidebar lists each folder's 100 largest items, followed by a row showing how many smaller items are hidden and how much space they take, with a **Show more** button.
- **Simpler Full Disk Access card.** The start screen now explains Full Disk Access in one line and two steps.

## v1.0.2

- **Copy Path.** A new inspector button copies the selected item's full path. It's also in the right-click menu and the Item menu (⌥⌘C).
- **Roomier inspector.** The inspector's actions are now two rows of equal-width buttons, with Move to Trash on its own row.

## v1.0.1

- **Intel Macs.** DiskU is now a universal app that runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
- **Scan a folder at launch.** `open DiskU.app --args ~/Downloads` opens DiskU and scans that folder.

## v1.0.0

- First release. Scan your home folder or any folder, see it as a treemap and a size-sorted outline, and inspect, Quick Look, reveal, or move items to the Trash.
